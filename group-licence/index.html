A few gentle answers

Who is it for?

Lifestyle and activity teams, and the managers who support them across more than one home, who want a month of activities planned without the weekly scramble.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Request the free December sample and you’ll receive it by email.

What do we need to run it?

A printer, or a TV or screen for the slides. The packs are ready to print or play.

Is it suitable for residents living with dementia?

Activities are marked with three levels and simple icons, so you can pick what suits each resident.

Do you need any resident information?

No. Please don’t send any through the sample form.

Who is behind it?

Luxe Years Co, an Australian business based in Melbourne. We create beautiful, practical activity packs, and we love hearing from activity teams.

Questions

Who is it for?

Activity coordinators, lifestyle teams and managers in aged-care homes who want a month of activities planned without the weekly scramble.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Request the free December sample and you'll receive it by email.

What do I need to run it?

A printer, or a TV or screen for the slides. The packs are ready to print or play.

Is it suitable for residents living with dementia?

Activities are marked with three levels and simple icons, so you can pick what suits each resident. Your team knows your residents best, so the pack is a tool to support your own judgement.

How do I pay?

We email a yearly invoice: A$129 per home, or A$99 per home for 2 or more homes in the same group.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. Just let us know by email.

Do you need any resident information?

No. We never ask for resident names or details, and please don't send any through the sample form.

Who is behind it?

Luxe Years Co, an Australian business based in Melbourne. We create beautiful, practical activity packs and we love hearing from activity teams.

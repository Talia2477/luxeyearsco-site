  • The full digital library of monthly packs — a fresh pack each month from January 2027, ready to print, with TV slides for group sessions.

A few gentle answers

How is this different from the group licence?

The group site licence (A$99 per home per year for 2 or more homes, or A$129 for a single home) covers the monthly packs. The Facility Toolkit adds the Outcome 7.1 evidence map and the full set of tick-and-go records, packaged as a self-serve toolkit for one facility.

Does this make our home compliant?

No. The toolkit supports your Outcome 7.1 evidence. It does not make a home compliant, and it does not replace your home’s own assessment, care planning or quality processes.

Do we need a call or onboarding session?

No. It’s self-serve. Everything arrives by email, and you can ask any question by email too.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Request the free December sample and you’ll receive it by email.

Do you need any resident information?

No. Records use initials and room numbers only, and we never ask for resident names or details.
